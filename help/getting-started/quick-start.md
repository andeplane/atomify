# Quick start

See a simulation running in two minutes, then keep it as a project.

::: tip First visit
On your very first visit, a short guided tour offers to walk you through your first simulation. Follow it, or close it and follow these steps instead. You can replay it any time with **Take the tour** in the sidebar.
:::

1. **Open Atomify** at [andeplane.github.io/atomify](https://andeplane.github.io/atomify/) in desktop Chrome or Firefox. The page reloads once on the first visit ([why](/getting-started/requirements#the-first-visit-reloads-once)).

2. **Wait for the engine.** The **Engine loading… NN%** chip at the bottom of the sidebar disappears when the engine is ready.

3. **Quick-run an example.** On the Home screen, click **Quick-run an example**. A simulation starts right away and you see the atoms move.

   ![The Home screen](/screenshots/home-welcome.webp)

4. **Look around.** Drag with the left mouse button to rotate, drag with the right button to pan, and scroll to zoom. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> to pause and again to resume. Press <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>9</kbd> to change the speed.

5. **Keep it.** A quick run is temporary. Click **Save as project** in the banner at the top to keep its files and the run.

   ![A completed quick run with the Save as project banner](/screenshots/quick-run-completed.webp)

6. **Run it again.** In your new project, click **Save & run simulation**. A new run starts and is recorded as run #002.

7. **Find your runs.** Open the **Runs** tab to see both runs, with their status and duration.

That's the whole loop: **pick or write a script, run it, look at the result, run again.**

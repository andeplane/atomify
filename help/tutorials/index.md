# Tutorials

The tutorials teach Atomify by doing. Each one is a complete, small project with every step spelled out. Do them in order if you are new: tutorials 1 to 4 build on each other. Tutorials 5 and 6 stand on their own.

| #   | Tutorial                                                        | Time   | You will learn                                                                                     |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | [Watch your first simulation](/tutorials/first-simulation)      | 10 min | Quick runs, moving the camera, pausing and speed, the console, live plots, saving as a project.    |
| 2   | [Write your own script](/tutorials/write-a-script)              | 15 min | Creating a project, writing and running a script, saving a camera view, run history and snapshots. |
| 3   | [Measure a diffusion coefficient](/tutorials/measure-diffusion) | 20 min | Computes and variables in live plots, CSV export, analyzing a run in the notebook.                 |
| 4   | [Sweep a parameter](/tutorials/parameter-sweep)                 | 20 min | Overridable variables, sweeps, the run queue, comparing runs in the notebook.                      |
| 5   | [Make a great-looking view](/tutorials/visualize-a-system)      | 20 min | Coloring by a compute or group, slicing, periodic images, measuring, rendering settings.           |
| 6   | [Share an example library](/tutorials/example-library)          | 20 min | The `examples.json` format, hosting it, and sharing a link that loads your library.                |

## Before you start

- Use desktop **Chrome** or **Firefox** ([requirements](/getting-started/requirements)).
- You don't need to know LAMMPS for tutorials 1 and 5. Tutorials 2 to 4 explain every line of the scripts they use; the [LAMMPS manual](https://docs.lammps.org/) has the details.
- The scripts are small enough to run in a minute or two on a laptop.

::: tip Copy buttons
Every script on these pages has a copy button in its top-right corner.
:::

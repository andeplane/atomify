## Where to start

<div class="start-grid">

**New to Atomify?** Read [What is Atomify?](/getting-started/what-is-atomify), check the [browser requirements](/getting-started/requirements), then do the [two-minute quick start](/getting-started/quick-start).

**Want to learn by doing?** The [tutorials](/tutorials/) take you from your first simulation to sweeps, notebook analysis and your own example library.

**Looking for something specific?** Use the search box (<kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>), the [how-to guides](/guide/runs), or the [reference](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts) for exact options, shortcuts and limits.

**Something not working?** See [troubleshooting](/faq/troubleshooting) and the [FAQ](/faq/).

</div>
